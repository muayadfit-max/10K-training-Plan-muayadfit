General Tips for 10K Beginners
Pacing is Everything:
 Always run at a conversation pace (Zone 2) for easy runs. You should be able to speak full sentences comfortably. If you gasp for breath, slow down or walk.
Listen to Your Body:
 Do not skip rest days. Muscle adaptation and cardiovascular growth happen during rest, not during the workout itself.
Cross Training:
 Focus on low-impact cardio such as cycling, swimming, elliptical training, or strength work. This builds aerobic capacity without overloading your joints.
Hydration & Nutrition:
 Drink plenty of water and maintain a balanced diet with proper macronutrient intake to power your runs and aid recovery.
