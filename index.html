Safety & Hot Climate Guidelines

Running in the Gulf requires specific adaptations. Follow these core principles for a safe training cycle.

Timing is Critical

Always schedule your runs for early morning (before 7 AM) or late evening to avoid peak sun and heat exhaustion.

Hydration Strategy

Don't wait until you're thirsty. Pre-hydrate adequately and use electrolyte supplements for runs lasting longer than 45 minutes.

Listen to Your Body

Muscle adaptation happens during rest. Never skip your scheduled rest days, and cross-train (cycle, swim) if your joints feel overloaded.

Pacing & Heart Rate

Run at a conversational pace (Zone 2). If you are gasping for breath, slow down or walk. Heat naturally increases your heart rate.
